What's inside

Everything in one free game, with no download or account needed.

Transforming robots

Every frame turns into its own vehicle. Transform Rush rams the enemy, and each class has its own weapon: heavy, medium or light.

40-mission story

Eight chapters, cutscenes, bosses with their own attacks, side missions with special goals, and a proper ending.

Build your robot

Mix head, torso, arms and legs from crates, pick powers, colors, patterns and stickers, then save your designs.

Boss creator

Blow up any custom robot to giant size, give it a rage phase and fight it, alone or with a friend at the controls.

Seasons and rewards

A 30-tier season track, weekly challenges, daily rewards, 74 achievements and mastery for every robot.

Plays anywhere

Keyboard with rebindable keys, game controllers, and touch controls on phones and tablets. Your progress saves on its own.

Screenshots

Taken straight from the game.

Modes

Story first, then whatever you feel like.

Campaign. 40 missions across 8 chapters, with Story, Normal and Hard difficulty.

Side missions. Escort a convoy, win with fists only, never block, and more.

Versus. Pick any two robots and fight the computer or a friend.

Arcade and Survival. Climb a ladder, or see how many fights you can last.

Horde. Waves of drones with a boss every fifth wave. Bring a partner.

Ranked, Daily Boss, Boss Rush. Climb tiers, beat today's boss, take on every boss in a row.

Co-op campaign and Tournament. Tag-team the story, or run a bracket for up to 8 players.

Mini-games. Car Smash, Target Range, Highway Run, Robot Soccer, King of the Hill and Dodge the Boss.

Training. A guided tutorial, plus a dummy to practise combos on.
